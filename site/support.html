Help & support

What is available now?

The browser app is available on phones and computers. The first module of all seven courses is free, and each account can start a seven-day trial of one full course. The iOS release and paid checkout are still in preparation. There is no payment needed to use the free content or trial.

Common questions

How do I restore my purchases on a new device?

For an existing iOS purchase, open the iOS app and go to Profile → Legal & Purchases → Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID you used to buy, and sign in to your B1nary account. Restore is not available in the browser. A four-course bundle restores its original course selections, rather than a new set.

If access is missing or still processing, reconnect and retry Restore purchases. Contact support with the product and affected courses if it continues. Do not buy the same product again to fix a restore problem.

I want a refund — what do I do?

All in-app purchases are processed by Apple, so refund requests go to Apple directly at reportaproblem.apple.com. We are not able to issue refunds on Apple's behalf.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile → Delete account. Registered users may need to verify their sign-in again. This permanently removes your account and the study data attached to it. See the privacy policy for detail on what is stored.

I'm not getting push notifications

Study reminders are a native-app feature; they are not sent by the browser app.

Check both places notifications can be switched off:

  • Your device settings — Settings → Notifications → B1nary.
  • The app — Profile → Notifications, where the master switch and each individual reminder can be turned on or off.

If permission was declined the first time you were asked, the system will not ask again — you have to enable it in device settings.

Do I need an account to use the app?

No. You can start as a guest and study the free first module of any course. If you create an account later, your progress, streak and badges carry over — you are upgraded in place rather than starting again.

How does the 7-day free trial work?

Open Profile → Legal & Purchases → Plans & Pricing, choose a course in the trial section, and tap Start 7-day free trial. You get every lesson and quiz in that course for seven days from activation. Each account gets one trial and the course cannot be changed after it starts. No card is required and there is no automatic charge. When it ends, your progress is kept and the first module of every course remains free. Downloads do not extend trial access. Paid checkout is not available yet.

How do I keep guest progress when I change devices?

On the original device, open Profile → Sign in or create an account to connect your guest study to an account. Use that same account on your other device. Clearing browser storage or losing an unlinked guest session can prevent you from recovering it.

Where can I find a course or topic?

Open Courses and search by course title or skill, such as subnetting, incident response or machine learning. You can also filter by subject. Clear the search and filters to see all seven courses again.

What can I study offline?

Open Profile → Learning → Download for offline to save lesson flashcards for courses you can access. Downloaded lessons and Recall practice work without a connection. Reconnect to load new quizzes, refresh access and save course progress. Downloads are stored on that device for your account and do not extend an expired trial.

How do Recall practice and Practice missed questions work?

In a lesson, choose Practice recall, explain the idea before revealing it, and mark cards that need another try. After a quiz, Practice missed questions starts a separate practice round with fresh answer ordering and explanations. It does not change the original score, completion or scheduled reviews. Choose Try again for a new full quiz attempt.

Where are my completion certificates?

Open Profile → Learning → My certificates to find and share certificates for courses you have completed. These record a B1nary study achievement and do not award an industry certification.

How do I send feedback or report a content mistake?

Use Profile → Support → Send feedback or email support. In-app feedback is private and separate from a public app rating. If sending fails, your message stays in the open form so you can retry. Include the course, module and question or card when reporting a content mistake.

The app is crashing or behaving oddly

Try force-closing the app and reopening it. If it keeps happening, email us with the details listed under Reporting bugs below.

Reporting bugs

Found something broken? Email support@binaryapp.org with:

  • Your device model and operating system version
  • Your browser and version, if you are using the website
  • Your app version, shown in Profile → Support → About B1nary, for an app issue
  • What you were doing when it happened
  • A screenshot, if you have one

Do not include passwords, sign-in codes or payment card details.

Course content questions

If you spot an error in a flashcard or quiz question, or have feedback on course content, tell us at support@binaryapp.org. We review all feedback and update content regularly.

Accessibility

If something in the app or on this site is difficult to use with a screen reader, keyboard, or at a larger text size, we want to know. See our accessibility statement for what we support and how to report a barrier.
