Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 April 2026 · Effective immediately

ByteStack ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use and safeguard your information when you use the ByteStack application.

Information we collect

Categories of data collected, what each contains, and why it is collected
CategoryWhat it includesWhy
Account data Name and email address, when you create an account To identify you and restore your progress across devices
Usage data Course progress, quiz scores, lesson completions, badges earned and streak data To run the features you use the app for
Device data Device type, operating system version and app version Debugging and diagnosing faults
Purchase data In-app purchase records processed through Apple's App Store To unlock what you bought. We never store payment card details

How we use your information

  • Provide and personalise your learning experience.
  • Track your progress across courses and modules.
  • Award badges and maintain streak records.
  • Send push notifications about your streak and new content — only if you grant permission.
  • Improve the app based on aggregated usage data.

Data storage

Your data is stored using Google Firebase, hosted in the United States. Firebase complies with GDPR and other major data protection regulations. We retain your data for as long as your account is active.

Third-party services

  • Google Firebase — authentication and cloud database.
  • RevenueCat — in-app purchase management.
  • Apple App Store — payment processing.

Each service has its own privacy policy. We do not sell your data to advertisers or any other third party.

Push notifications

We may send notifications to remind you about your streak or tell you about new content. Notifications are off until you allow them, and you can turn them off at any time — either in your device settings, or in the app under Profile → Notifications, where each type of reminder can be switched on or off independently.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of your data.
  • Delete your account at any time in the app under Profile → Delete Account.
  • Withdraw consent for push notifications at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, email support@binaryapp.org. Deleting your account from inside the app removes your account and the study data attached to it.

Children's privacy

ByteStack is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe we have inadvertently done so, please contact us immediately.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact us

Questions about this policy? Email support@binaryapp.org.
