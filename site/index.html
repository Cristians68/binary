How it works

A clear path from understanding a concept to practicing it from memory.

1. Learn, then recall

Read a focused lesson with examples. Open Recall practice to hide the explanation, try it from memory, and check what you remembered.

2. Learn from each answer

Answer a quiz with shuffled options and immediate explanations. See your pass mark, inspect mistakes, and practice just the questions you missed.

3. Return for review

Missed quiz questions enter spaced review. An immediate practice round leaves that schedule intact, so you can check your recall again after some time has passed.

Common questions

Is this an official certification course?

No. B1nary is an independent study aid. It is not accredited by, affiliated with, or endorsed by any certification body, and completing a course here does not award an industry certification. It is built to help you prepare for one. Each course card above names the trademark holder for the exam it relates to.

Is it a subscription?

No. The 7-day trial ends without charging you and does not renew. The planned iOS purchase options are one-time, non-consumable purchases. Everything includes future courses. Paid checkout is not available yet.

What happens when my free trial ends?

Full access to your chosen trial course ends after seven days. Your progress is kept, and the first module of every course remains free. Downloads do not extend the trial. Each account gets one trial, and its course cannot be changed after activation.

Is the iPhone app available?

The iOS release is in preparation. You can use the browser app now on your phone or computer. This website will link to the App Store when the release is available; there is no public download link yet.

Do I need an account to try it?

No. You can open the app and start the free first module of any course as a guest. If you later create an account, your progress and streak carry across — you are upgraded in place rather than starting again.

Can I study offline?

Download lesson flashcards for courses you can access, then read them and use Recall practice without a connection. Reconnect to load new quizzes, refresh course access, and save course progress. Downloads and review queues are stored on the device for your account.

Does focused practice change my quiz score?

No. Practice missed questions opens a separate round with just the questions you missed. It does not change your original result, award course completion, or postpone your scheduled reviews. Choose Try again in quiz results to take a new full attempt.

Can I use these study tools on the website?

Yes. Select Open the app to use the browser app, including lessons, Recall practice, quizzes, focused practice, and spaced review. Light and dark themes and layouts for phones and larger screens are available in the app.

What happens to my purchase if I change device?

For existing iOS purchases, use the same Apple ID and open Profile → Legal & Purchases → Restore purchases in the iOS app. Restore is not available in the browser. Four-course bundles retain their original selections. Sign in to the same B1nary account to recover synced progress, streaks and badges. Contact support if access is not restored.

How do I delete my account and data?

There is a Delete account option inside the app under Profile. It removes your account and the study data attached to it. See the privacy policy for exactly what is stored and for how long.
