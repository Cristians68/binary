Help & support

Questions, a bug, or a problem with a purchase — this page covers the common ones, and we answer email.

Contact us

support@binaryapp.org
We typically reply within 1–2 business days.

Common questions

How do I restore my purchases on a new device?

Open the app and go to Profile → Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy.

I want a refund — what do I do?

All in-app purchases are processed by Apple, so refund requests go to Apple directly at reportaproblem.apple.com. We are not able to issue refunds on Apple's behalf.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and the study data attached to it. See the privacy policy for detail on what is stored.

I'm not getting push notifications

Check both places notifications can be switched off:

  • Your device settings — Settings → Notifications → B1nary.
  • The app — Profile → Notifications, where the master switch and each individual reminder can be turned on or off.

If permission was declined the first time you were asked, the system will not ask again — you have to enable it in device settings.

Do I need an account to use the app?

No. You can start as a guest and study the free first module of any course. If you create an account later, your progress, streak and badges carry over — you are upgraded in place rather than starting again.

The app is crashing or behaving oddly

Try force-closing the app and reopening it. If it keeps happening, email us with the details listed under Reporting bugs below.

Reporting bugs

Found something broken? Email support@binaryapp.org with:

  • Your device model (for example, iPhone 15 Pro)
  • Your OS version (Settings → General → About)
  • What you were doing when it happened
  • A screenshot, if you have one

Course content questions

If you spot an error in a flashcard or quiz question, or have feedback on course content, tell us at support@binaryapp.org. We review all feedback and update content regularly.

Accessibility

If something in the app or on this site is difficult to use with a screen reader, keyboard, or at a larger text size, we want to know. See our accessibility statement for what we support and how to report a barrier.
