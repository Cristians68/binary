How it works

Three steps, repeated until the material sticks.

1. Learn the concept

Each module is a set of flashcards covering one topic, written in plain language with worked scenarios and the traps exams like to set.

2. Prove it on the quiz

Every module ends in a quiz with shuffled answers, so passing means you know the material rather than the position of the right option.

3. Keep the streak

Daily goals, streaks and badges track consistency — the thing that actually decides whether you finish a certification.

Common questions

Is this an official certification course?

No. B1nary Academy is an independent study aid. It is not accredited by, affiliated with, or endorsed by any certification body, and completing a course here does not award an industry certification. It is built to help you prepare for one. Each course card above names the trademark holder for the exam it relates to.

Is it a subscription?

No. Every purchase is a one-time payment and access does not expire. If you buy the all-courses bundle, future courses are included at no extra cost.

Do I need an account to try it?

No. You can open the app and start the free first module of any course as a guest. If you later create an account, your progress and streak carry across — you are upgraded in place rather than starting again.

Can I study offline?

Yes. Courses you have access to can be downloaded to your device and studied without a connection. Your progress syncs the next time you are online.

What happens to my purchase if I change device?

Purchases are tied to your store account, so "Restore purchases" in the app brings them back on a new device. Signing in with the same account also restores your progress, streak and badges.

How do I delete my account and data?

There is a Delete account option inside the app under Profile. It removes your account and the study data attached to it. See the privacy policy for exactly what is stored and for how long.
